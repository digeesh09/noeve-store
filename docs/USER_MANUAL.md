## 1. For Customers (Web & Mobile Store)

The Customer Store is available via our Web Portal and Native Mobile Application (iOS & Android). Both platforms offer a synchronized and seamless experience.

### 1.1 Creating an Account & Logging In
1. **Sign Up:** Click the "Sign Up" or profile icon in the navigation bar. Provide your email, full name, and a secure password.
2. **Log In:** Use your registered email and password. Once logged in, your cart and preferences will sync across both web and mobile platforms.
3. **Profile Management:** Navigate to the "Account" section to update your personal details, manage saved shipping addresses, and change your password.

### 1.2 Browsing and Searching for Products
- **Categories:** Use the main navigation menu to browse categories such as Jewellery, Care Products, Accessories, etc.
- **Product Details:** Click on any product to view high-resolution images, detailed descriptions, pricing, available variants (e.g., size, material), and care instructions.

### 1.3 Shopping Cart & Checkout
1. **Add to Cart:** Select your preferred variant (if applicable) and click "Add to Cart". A notification will confirm the addition.
2. **Review Cart:** Click the Cart icon to view your selected items, update quantities, or remove products.
3. **Checkout:** Proceed to checkout, select or add a new shipping address, and choose your preferred payment method.
4. **Order Confirmation:** Upon successful payment, you will receive an order confirmation email along with a unique Order ID.

### 1.4 Managing Orders
- **Order History:** Visit the "Account" > "Orders" section to view your past and current orders.
- **Order Tracking:** Click on an active order to view its current status (e.g., Processing, Shipped, Delivered).

## 2. For Administrators (Web Admin Dashboard)

The Web Admin Dashboard is restricted to authorized personnel and is used to manage the store's backend operations.

### 2.1 Accessing the Dashboard
- Navigate to the designated Admin URL.
- Log in using your Administrator credentials. (Note: Only accounts with `ADMIN` roles can access this portal).

### 2.2 Managing the Catalog (Products & Categories)
- **View Products:** Navigate to the "Products" tab to see a list of all items.
- **Add New Product:** Click "Create Product". Fill in the required fields including Name, Description, Price, Stock Quantity, Category, and upload product images.
- **Edit/Delete:** Use the action buttons next to a product to update stock levels, change prices, or remove an item from the catalog.

### 2.3 Managing Orders
- **Order Queue:** The "Orders" tab displays all customer orders in real-time.
- **Order Details:** Click on an order to view the purchased items, customer details, and shipping address.
- **Update Status:** As an order progresses through fulfillment, update its status (e.g., from *Confirmed* to *Processing*, then *Shipped*). Status changes will automatically reflect on the customer's end.

### 2.4 User & Inventory Management
- **Customers:** View registered customer profiles and their order histories.
- **Inventory Monitoring:** Keep track of stock levels across all products to ensure items do not oversell. Update inventory counts as new stock arrives.

## 3. Support and Troubleshooting

### 3.1 Common Issues
- **Forgot Password:** Use the "Forgot Password" link on the login screen to receive a secure reset link via email.
- **Payment Failures:** Ensure your card details are correct. If the issue persists, please try an alternative payment method or contact your bank.
- **Mobile App Not Syncing:** Ensure you have a stable internet connection and try pulling down to refresh the page.

### 3.2 Contacting Support
If you encounter any persistent issues or require further assistance, please contact our support team:
- **Email:** support@noeve.store
- **Grievance Redressal:** Visit our Grievance Redressal page for escalated concerns.
